Free lesson, right now

The 60-second niche test (use it even if you never buy anything from me)

Before you make a single video, ask four questions about your niche. If it fails any of them, walk away. It'll save you months.

Question 1

What does it pay?

Some niches earn $8 or more per 1,000 views. Others earn under $1. Same work, very different paycheck.

Question 2

Is there demand?

How many views a month do channels in it actually get? No views, no money.

Question 3

Are NEW channels winning?

This is the big one. If only old channels get views, the door is closed. Skip it.

Question 4

Can you make it every week?

A niche that needs 40 hours of animation per video isn't a niche. It's a second job.
